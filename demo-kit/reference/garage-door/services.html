Garage door repair

When the door stops working

Most calls start the same way: the door made a bang, it is sitting crooked, it goes part way and stops, or the opener runs and nothing moves. Describe what you see and hear. That is enough to get started.

Typical repairs

  • Broken torsion or extension springs
  • Snapped or frayed cables
  • Worn rollers and hinges
  • Bent or misaligned tracks
  • Damaged panels
  • Doors off their track

What to tell us

  • Is the door open or shut?
  • Is a car stuck inside?
  • Any loud bang or new noise?
  • Does the opener run?

If a spring or cable looks broken, do not try to lift the door. Keep clear of it until it has been looked at.

New door installation

A new door, fitted and set up

For a door that is past repair, or a garage that has never had one. We measure the opening, talk through the choices with you, then remove the old door, fit the new one and balance it so it runs smoothly.

Choices we go through

  • Size and headroom
  • Panel style
  • Windows
  • Material and colour
  • Insulation
  • Opener and remotes

What happens on the day

  • Old door removed
  • Tracks and springs fitted
  • Door balanced and tested

Opener installation

Open it from the car

A new opener for a door that is still lifted by hand, or a replacement for one that has stopped working. We fit the motor and rail, connect the door, then set up the remotes and the wall control with you.

Included in the set-up

  • Motor and rail
  • Wall control
  • Remotes
  • Safety sensors
  • Travel limits and force

Good to know

  • The door must run freely first
  • A power point is needed near the motor
