My door will not open. What should I do first?

Stop using the opener and do not try to lift the door by hand if a spring or cable looks broken. Keep people and cars clear of it, then call and describe what you see.

Should I repair the door or replace it?

It depends on the age of the door, the condition of the panels and hardware, and what has failed. We look at it and explain both options so you can decide.

Can you replace only the opener?

Yes. If the door itself runs freely and is in good condition, an opener can be replaced on its own.
