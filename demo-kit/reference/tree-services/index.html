Do I need permission to remove a tree?

Some trees are protected by a preservation order, sit in a conservation area, or are covered by planning conditions. Check with your local planning authority before booking, and mention anything you know when you request a quote so it can be factored in.

What happens to the wood and the grindings?

That is agreed in the quote. Timber can be cut into lengths and left, or taken away. Grindings can be left to settle, spread on borders or removed.

Can a stump be ground near a wall, fence or patio?

Often, depending on access and what is underground. This is checked during the site assessment, and the quote says what is possible and to what depth.

What should I do about a tree damaged in a storm?

Keep people and vehicles away from it and do not try to cut or pull it yourself. Send a photo with your request if it is safe to take one, and say whether the tree is touching a building, road or power line.
